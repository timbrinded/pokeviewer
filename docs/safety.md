# Safety for an adult-built child-adjacent gadget

Pokeviewer source and the Waveshare development board are not a certified,
enclosed, impact-tested, waterproof, fire-tested, or regulatory-approved
children's toy. An adult is responsible for the cell, wiring, enclosure,
assembly, inspection, charging, placement, and supervision.

## Battery and charging

- Use only a reputable protected single-cell 3.7 V lithium battery compatible
  with the board's charger and connector.
- Charge only while an adult is present, on a stable non-combustible surface,
  away from bedding, soft furnishings, heat, liquids, direct sunlight, and
  escape routes. Do not charge overnight.
- Do not use an incompatible charger, damaged cable, loose connector, bare
  cell, improvised series/parallel pack, or unprotected cell.
- Stop use and charging immediately if the cell or board is swollen, hot,
  leaking, hissing, smelling unusual, physically damaged, wet, or behaving
  unexpectedly. Do not puncture, compress, open, or modify the cell.
- If there is smoke, fire, or venting, move people away, leave the area, and
  call emergency services. Do not risk handling a failing cell.

Poorly designed, damaged, overheated, or electrically abused lithium-ion
systems can enter thermal runaway and release flammable or toxic gases.
Current official guidance includes the
[UK Office for Product Safety and Standards battery guidance][opss] and
[London Fire Brigade battery and charger advice][lfb].

## Mechanical and child safety

- A four-year-old must not have access to the bare development board, battery,
  connectors, headers, screws, magnets, loose wire, sharp edges, or charging
  cable. These can present small-parts, choking, ingestion, strangulation,
  cut, short-circuit, and burn hazards.
- Use an adult-assembled enclosure that cannot be opened without a tool, keeps
  the cell mechanically restrained and electrically insulated, protects the
  display, provides strain relief, and does not trap unsafe heat.
- Inspect the enclosure, cable, display, cell, and fasteners before every use.
  Stop use after a drop, crush, liquid exposure, loose part, cracked enclosure,
  damaged display, or accessible wire.
- Place the device where it cannot fall into a cot/bed, bath, water, heater, or
  walkway. Keep charging equipment out of the child's reach.
- Supervise use. Do not let a child charge, connect, repair, sleep with, carry
  by its cable, or put the device or parts in their mouth.

The project does not certify an enclosure, battery, charger, cable, or finished
assembly. Anyone supplying a finished product must perform the applicable
safety, legal, and conformity work rather than relying on this repository.
