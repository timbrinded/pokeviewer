# Crystal sprite tone-mapping evidence

These images support
[ADR 0012](../../decisions/0012-show-generations-one-and-two-with-shaded-crystal-sprites.md).
Both are 2× nearest-neighbour enlargements of one-bit panel pixels, so each
panel pixel is a 2 × 2 block. They were made during review with a throwaway
script and are not regenerated by `xtask`. The final column of
`tone-mapping-options.png` matches the committed renderer pixel for pixel,
which was checked against the Umbreon, Vileplume, Celebi, and Charizard
goldens.

## `yellow-v1-vs-crystal-v2.png`

Left: the v2.0.0 golden cards (Yellow sprite, two darkest colours black).
Right: the same Pokémon and weekday with the Crystal sprite and shaded
rendering. Rows: Bulbasaur, Charizard, Pikachu, Aerodactyl, Mr. Mime,
Nidoran♀.

## `tone-mapping-options.png`

Rows: Vileplume (45), Gengar (94), Umbreon (197), Snorlax (143), Tyranitar
(248). Columns, all from the same Crystal source:

1. the v1 rule: the two darkest colours black, the two lightest white;
2. four shades as 0, 1, 2, and 4 black pixels per 2 × 2 cell; and
3. option 2 with black interiors at 3 of 4 pixels, which is the committed
   renderer.

Under the v1 rule, 39 of the 251 Crystal sprites are more than 80 % ink
inside their outline. Vileplume's body and face and most of Gengar, Umbreon,
and Murkrow become solid black, and Snorlax loses its belly. With option 3,
the darkest sprite is about 79 % ink. Light bodies, such as Pikachu, read as
a 25 % stipple instead of disappearing into the white background.

Physical-panel review is still pending. At about 184 pixels per inch, single
dot patterns should read as grey at arm's length; confirm on the device.

The images contain no child, device, host-path, or USB-identifying data.
